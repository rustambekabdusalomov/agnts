What the video shows

A developer hands agnts a ticket and only steps in to answer and approve. Everything in the video is the working product at agnts.uz.

  1. Step 1

    Label a ticket

    A ticket in Jira gets the agnts label. agnts picks it up and reads the ticket and the codebase.

  2. Step 2

    Answer its questions

    Anything unclear becomes a short question with options, answered in one click.

  3. Step 3

    agnts codes and tests

    In a fresh container on its own branch. The live log shows the build, lint and tests passing.

  4. Step 4

    Review the result

    The summary, the diff, the AI review findings and, for front-end work, screenshots of the changed pages.

  5. Step 5

    Approve

    agnts pushes, opens the pull request and moves the ticket. Or request changes, and agnts fixes them and learns from it.

  6. Step 6

    Learning

    After the push, agnts reads the change and turns anything new in it (a pattern, an algorithm, a library) into a lesson with an explanation, code references and a short quiz. The developer grows with every ticket, not just the codebase.
