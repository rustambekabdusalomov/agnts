# Analytics

How well hiro works for you over time. Pick 7, 30 or 90 days; every number is compared with the period before it.

## Key numbers

Tickets pushed, success rate, approval on the first try, hiro's time per ticket, time waiting on you, and turns per ticket. Green changes are improvements.

## Throughput and outcomes

Tickets per day and how they ended: approved first time, approved after changes, rejected or stopped.

## Speed and time

Minutes hiro works versus minutes tickets wait for you, and which step takes the longest.

## Why runs stop

Failing tests, plan limits, your stops and turn limits, ranked.

## Models and repositories

Runs, success and plan cost per model, and how each repository performs, including the effect of CLAUDE.md.

## Insights

Short conclusions drawn from the numbers, with what to change.
