# Dashboard

Your starting point. What hiro is doing right now, what is waiting on you, and how this week compares with the last.

## Top cards

Needs you, runs in progress, pushes this week and your plan session. Click a card to open the matching list or page.

## Waiting on you

Tickets that need answers, a review or a retry, oldest first, with how long they've waited. Clearing these keeps hiro busy.

## Live runs

Each running ticket with its model, turns used against the limit, time in the current step and the latest log line.

## Plan usage

Session and weekly usage, when they reset, and roughly how many more runs fit in this session.

## Pipeline and today

Open tickets by step, and today's events by hour so you can see what happened overnight.

## Repositories and activity

Open work and the latest ticket per repository, plus the most recent events.
