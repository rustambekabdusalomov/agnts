# Ticket lists

Everything hiro is handling, split by where it stands. The sidebar filters are shortcuts to the same list.

## Needs you

Your inbox. Tickets wait here when hiro has questions, has finished changes for review, stopped, or paused at the plan limit. Nothing moves forward until you act.

## Running

Tickets hiro is working on right now, each in its own container on your server.

## In queue

Jira tickets with the hiro label and tasks you saved for later. Open one and press Analyze ticket to start.

## Finished

Pushed or rejected work. Pushed tickets have a pull request, or went straight to main in repositories where you allowed it.

## Source

Jira means the ticket came from Jira and hiro updates its status there. Task means you created it in hiro.

## Search

The search box at the top filters by ticket key or title.
